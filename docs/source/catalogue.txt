Built for Vape Stores

60% of our clients are vape shops. Here's what they need, and what to watch

What vape shops buy most (our read)

     Customers are repeat buyers (weekly juice, coils and pods), so loyalty and restock messages earn more here than in most
     shops.

     The counter questions are 'what fits my device?' and 'what flavour should I try?', so the Coil & Pod Finder and Flavour Finder
     save staff time.

     Owners lose money on faulty coils and dead flavours: the Stock & Warranty Tracker and Monthly Growth Report pay for
     themselves.

Compliance and platform checklist

Law is changing  The Tobacco Products and Electronic Delivery Systems Control Bill was not yet law at the last check
                 (committee stage, June 2026). It would ban vape advertising, promotion and retail displays. Our
                 tools are the shop's own pages and normal chats, not ads, and are easy to adjust.

WhatsApp rules   Third-party guides say vapes cannot be sold through the WhatsApp Business API or the in-app
                 catalogue. So the vape storefront is our own web page with an ordinary chat link, and we do not
                 build AI bots on the Business API for vape shops. Confirm against WhatsApp's own current policy
                 before launch.

Ad and social platforms Meta, TikTok and Google restrict vape ads and promotion. So for vape shops, Profile Setup means
                                            information pages only, and we don't sell ad management. We rely on Google Maps, opt-in lists,

                                            loyalty and QR stands instead.

Age and consent  The 18+ pop-up, checkout confirmation, consent wording and ID-check reminder are in the 18+ Age
                 Check & Consent Setup (#2). It is a self-declaration. The owner stays responsible for ID checks at
                 collection or delivery.

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice. Ask the shop's attorney to confirm wording and local rules.

What We Recommend

How I would price and sell, based on the market data

     1. Keep setup low and earn on the monthly
      Small shops resist big upfront costs. Our pack setups are R2,200 to R7,000 (3 instalments available), and the monthly plan
      (R250 to R800) is where the revenue builds. Compare with R6,000 to R25,000 for a basic online store (ProCompare).

     2. Use the Health Check as the door-opener
      Offer a free sample report, or sell it at R1,800 (free inside Vape Complete and Retail Online Shop). Agencies charge R10,000+
      for a technical SEO audit, so R1,800 for a local snapshot is easy to defend.

     3. Charge a small fee for the 18+ check
      R600 covers real set-up and testing, plus R80 a month if the shop wants consent records kept. Show its value inside each
      vape pack so owners see it isn't a piece of paper. Remember it is a self-declaration, not proof of age.

     4. Photography is cheaper than the market
      Studio photographers charge about R200 to R280 per product for 11 to 25 products (ProCompare). R2,500 for up to 20
      products plus shop photos is good value and still profitable on a phone shoot.

     5. Push packs and the 6-month minimum
      Packs give shops a lower price and free edits, and give you steady income. Keep the 6-month minimum, offer 2 months
      free for 12 months prepaid, and remember that domain and email are the only yearly bills, paid at cost.

     6. Keep vape tools safe
      Use our own web pages and plain chat links. Skip the WhatsApp catalogue, AI bots on the Business API and ad services for
      vape shops. Re-check the Bill and the platform policies every quarter.

Sales Cheat Sheet

For you and your partner: how to pitch, and what to say when they push back

The 30-second pitch

"We help local shops get found on Google and take orders on WhatsApp. For vape shops we build a simple website with a
coil and flavour finder, an age check and a loyalty card. A pack's setup starts from R2,200 and you can pay it in three
instalments, then a small monthly fee covers hosting, support and free edits. Can I send you a free Health Check that shows
how you compare with the shops around you?"

Objections and answers

"It's too expensive."

Start with Vape Starter or Retail Starter: setup from R2,200, or 3 instalments of about R730 to R1,330. Compare it with R6,000 to
R25,000 for a basic online store elsewhere.

"I don't want a monthly fee."

The monthly pays for hosting, security, support and up to 5 free edits. Edits alone are R120 each otherwise. There is a 6-month
minimum, then month to month.

"I already have a Facebook page."

Great, we keep it. A page can't take structured orders, show stock or run an age check, and vape ad rules are tightening. Your
own site and Google profile are yours.

"Can I just do it myself?"

You can, and DIY builders cost about R580 a month plus your time. We save the time and add vape-specific tools. Offer the free
Health Check as a no-risk first step.

"What if I cancel?"
Monthly services stop at the end of the notice month. Once-off items stay yours.

     "Is the age check legal proof?"
     No. It is a self-declaration plus reminders. The shop still checks ID at collection or delivery, and should confirm wording with its
     attorney.
